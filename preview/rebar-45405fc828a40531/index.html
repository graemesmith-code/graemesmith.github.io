Methodology, budget context and illustrations

REBAR Methodology

Researchers from Kitware, the University of Pennsylvania, Duality Robotics and partners translated the Defense Department's five Responsible AI principles into 43 key attributes, 121 behaviors and 135 measurable observables. Using an LLM-assisted generator (Prose2JSON), they produced 215 simulation scenarios spanning 12 ethical-difficulty levels inside Duality Robotics' Unreal Engine-based FalconSim, evaluating a non-lethal quadrotor search-and-report algorithm—not a weapon firing.

Pentagon Budget Context

REBAR was developed under DARPA's $22.8 million ASIMOV program. In DARPA's June 2025 budget justification, ASIMOV explicitly named the Pentagon's independent testing office (DOT&E) and the military services as recipients. By April 2026, the budget book kept law-of-war language intact but dropped the named handover to military testers—pivoting to general “future autonomous-systems R&D” with FY2026–27 funding listed as dashes and roughly $19 million in Phase II options unexercised.

About These Visuals
